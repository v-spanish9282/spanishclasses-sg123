    Real teachers, real rooms

    Spanish is learned face to face.

    A language lives in conversation. Across a table, a good teacher notices the moment a student hesitates, hears the sound they cannot quite make, and knows exactly what to say next.

    That is what our teachers do. A native speaker sits with your child, listens, corrects and encourages, and builds the kind of confidence that only comes from talking to a real person.

    They bring the culture

    For IB families

    Teachers who have already helped students like yours.

    Our teachers have taught IB Spanish to students from Singapore's international schools, including SAS, Nexus and UWC. They know the syllabus, the assessments and the pressure that comes with them.

    Whether your child is in MYP Language Acquisition, DP Spanish ab initio or Spanish B, lessons follow what they study at school and what the exam asks of them. Speaking comes first, so the individual oral feels familiar by exam day.

    Individual oral practice

    Regular speaking practice with a native teacher, in the format the IB uses.

    Writing with clear feedback

    Exam-style writing tasks, marked with notes on exactly what to fix.

    Listening and reading

    Real Spanish audio and texts, practised the way the exam tests them.
